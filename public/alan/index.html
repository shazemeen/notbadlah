Year by year

Why it keeps growing

A client who pays for 20 years pays you every year they stay. This is what you earn on their premium each policy year, commission and BSC together, on investment-linked plans of 20 years or more.

So the clients you close this month are still paying you in year five, on top of everyone you close after them.

Words you'll hear

AFYP
Annualised first-year premium: what a client pays you in their first year. RM417 a month is RM5,000 AFYP.
AFYC
Annualised first-year commission: 25% of AFYP. The programme tiers are measured on this. RM20,000 AFYP is RM5,000 AFYC.
Commission + BSC
What you earn on every premium your clients pay. BSC is the Balanced Scorecard bonus, paid with your commission.
NGM
Next Gen Millionaire: the 24-month full-time programme that pays a monthly bonus on top of commission.
Case
One policy sold to a client.
Persistency
How many of your clients are still paying a year later. 90% means 9 in every 10. At 90%, 81 in 100 are still paying in year 3.
Renewal
A client's premium in year 2 and after. You keep earning on it.
Ascend
A yearly bonus, paid every February, based on how much you sold the year before.

How this is worked out

  • Commission + BSC rates are from the Agency Compensation Handbook v7.4 (March 2023), investment-linked regular premium, 20 years or more. Year 1 is 17.5% + 7.5%, year 2 is 12.5% + 12.5%, year 3 is 10% + 9%; the BSC table stops at year 3, so years 4 to 6 are commission only. BSC is taken at a 100% score.
  • Commission + BSC is paid on premiums collected, the month after they are collected. A monthly payer pays two months when they sign up, then monthly again from month 3; a yearly payer pays the full year up front.
  • Programme bonus is paid the month after. A steady plan never needs the quarterly catch-up.
  • Ascend follows the handbook's Ascend rules, assumed to carry on each year: paid every February on the year before, using that year's total sales. New Life Planners need only 70% of each tier in their first two calendar years. Only premiums collected within each year are counted, to stay conservative.
  • This is a projection of one steady plan, not a promise. Actual pay depends on policies being validated and kept in force.
